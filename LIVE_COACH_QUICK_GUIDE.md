# 🤖 Live AI Coach - Quick Reference

## What is it?
A real-time assistant that helps you improve your answers **while you're typing them**, like having an expert coach beside you during the interview.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Start Practice Interview
Click "Start Practice Interview" on the home page.

### Step 2: Begin Answering
Start typing your answer in the text box.

### Step 3: Watch the Coach
The Live AI Coach panel (right side) will activate and show suggestions in real-time.

### Step 4: Follow Guidance
Read the suggestions and adjust your answer accordingly.

### Step 5: Submit Better Answer
Click "Next Question" when satisfied with your improved response.

## What the Coach Checks

### ✅ Answer Length
- Too short? → Add more details
- Just right? → Keep going!
- Too long? → Stay focused

### ✅ STAR Method (for behavioral questions)
- **S**ituation: Set the context
- **T**ask: Explain the challenge
- **A**ction: What YOU did
- **R**esult: The outcome (use numbers!)

### ✅ Communication Quality
- Filler words (um, uh, like)
- First-person language
- Enthusiasm
- Specific examples
- Metrics and numbers

### ✅ Question-Specific Tips
Different guidance for:
- Introduction
- Strengths/Weaknesses
- Challenges
- Company questions
- Career goals

## Pro Tips

### 🎯 Best Practices
1. **Read suggestions while typing** - Don't wait until done
2. **Follow STAR structure** for behavioral questions
3. **Add metrics** whenever possible (%, $, time)
4. **Show enthusiasm** with positive language
5. **Be specific** with examples

### ⚡ Quick Wins
- Replace "um" with brief pauses
- Use "I" statements (I did, I achieved)
- Add numbers (saved $50K, improved 30%)
- Mention company values
- End with results/outcomes

### 🚫 Avoid
- Filler words (um, uh, like, basically)
- Vague statements without examples
- Rambling answers over 3 minutes
- Cliché weaknesses ("I'm a perfectionist")
- Missing STAR components

## Example Session

**Question:** "Describe a challenge you faced."

**You type:** "I faced a challenge"
**Coach says:** 💡 Start with SITUATION: When and where?

**You add:** "at my last job when project was delayed"
**Coach says:** 💡 Now the ACTION: What did YOU do?

**You continue:** "I organized daily meetings"
**Coach says:** 💡 Add RESULT: Use numbers!

**You finish:** "We finished 2 weeks early, saved $50K"
**Coach says:** ✅ Excellent! Great STAR structure!

## Settings

### Always Active
The coach is always on during practice - no need to enable.

### Update Frequency
Suggestions update every 0.8 seconds as you type.

### Message Limit
Max 3 suggestions shown at once (prioritized automatically).

## Troubleshooting

### Coach Not Showing?
- Make sure you're typing in the answer field
- Check that you're in a practice session
- Refresh if needed

### Too Many Suggestions?
- Focus on the top one first
- Suggestions auto-prioritize importance
- Only 3 shown at a time

### Suggestions Not Relevant?
- Keep typing - they adapt as you write
- STAR prompts are for behavioral questions only
- General tips apply to all answers
