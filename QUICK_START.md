# ⚡ Quick Start Guide

Get up and running with AI Interview Coach in 2 minutes!

## 🎯 Prerequisites

Make sure you have:
- ✅ Node.js installed (version 16 or higher)
- ✅ A working webcam
- ✅ A microphone
- ✅ A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari)

## 🚀 Launch the App

### Windows Users

**Easiest way:**
1. Double-click `start.bat`
2. Wait for the browser to open automatically
3. That's it! 🎉

**Alternative:**
1. Open Command Prompt or PowerShell
2. Navigate to the project folder
3. Run: `npm run dev`
4. Open http://localhost:3000 in your browser

### Mac/Linux Users

1. Open Terminal
2. Navigate to the project folder
3. Run: `npm run dev`
4. Open http://localhost:3000 in your browser

## 📱 Using the Application

### Step 1: Home Screen
You'll see the welcome screen with three feature cards:
- 🎥 Video Analysis
- 📈 Speech Analysis  
- 🏆 AI Feedback

Click **"Start Practice Interview"** button.

### Step 2: Grant Permissions
Your browser will ask for permissions:
- **Allow Camera** - Required for video analysis
- **Allow Microphone** - Required for audio feedback

Click "Allow" for both.

### Step 3: Interview Session

You'll see:
- **Your video feed** (top)
- **Current question** (middle)
- **Answer text area** (bottom)
- **Tips panel** (right side)

**To answer a question:**
1. Read the question carefully
2. Type your answer (or speak - just type what you say)
3. Click **"Next Question"** when done
4. The AI will analyze your response
5. Continue to the next question

**Controls:**
- 📹 Video On/Off - Toggle camera
- 🎤 Mic On/Off - Toggle microphone
- 🏠 End Interview - Go back to home

### Step 4: Review Results

After completing all questions, you'll see:

**Overall Scores:**
- 🏆 Overall Score
- 📊 Content Quality
- 👁️ Eye Contact
- 🎤 Speech Clarity

**Detailed Analysis:**
- Question-by-question breakdown
- Performance metrics for each answer
- Confidence, clarity, and relevance scores
- Sentiment analysis

**Actions:**
- 📈 Show Advanced Analytics - View performance timeline
- 💾 Export Results - Download your session data
- 🏠 Back to Home - Start a new session

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Before You Start
- 🪟 **Test your setup**: Click the camera/mic buttons to ensure they work
- 💡 **Good lighting**: Face a window or lamp for better video analysis
- 🎧 **Quiet space**: Minimize background noise
- 👔 **Dress professionally**: Practice like it's the real thing

### During the Interview
- 👀 **Look at the camera**: Not at yourself on screen
- 🗣️ **Speak clearly**: Moderate pace, clear pronunciation
- ⏱️ **Take your time**: Pause to think before answering
- 📝 **Be detailed**: Aim for 2-3 minute answers
- ✨ **Use STAR method**: Situation, Task, Action, Result

### After the Interview
- 📊 **Review all metrics**: Don't just look at the overall score
- 📝 **Read recommendations**: They're tailored to your performance
- 🔄 **Practice regularly**: Track improvement over time
- 💾 **Export results**: Keep a record of your progress

## 🎯 Sample Workflow

**First Session (20-30 minutes):**
1. Launch app and grant permissions (2 min)
2. Complete all 10 questions (15-20 min)
3. Review results and recommendations (5-8 min)
4. Export results for later review

**Regular Practice (Weekly):**
1. Review previous session's weak points
2. Do a new practice session
3. Compare results with previous sessions
4. Focus on specific areas needing improvement

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### Camera Not Working
- ✅ Check browser permissions (click lock icon in address bar)
- ✅ Close other apps using the camera (Zoom, Skype, etc.)
- ✅ Try a different browser
- ✅ Restart your browser

### Microphone Not Working
- ✅ Check browser permissions
- ✅ Check system sound settings
- ✅ Ensure microphone is not muted
- ✅ Try a different microphone/headset

### Page Won't Load
- ✅ Make sure dev server is running (check terminal)
- ✅ Try http://localhost:3000 directly
- ✅ Clear browser cache and reload
- ✅ Restart the dev server

### Performance Issues
- ✅ Close unnecessary browser tabs
- ✅ Close other heavy applications
- ✅ Use Chrome or Edge (best performance)
- ✅ Restart your computer if needed

## ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `Enter` | Move to next question (when on answer field) |
| `Esc` | Show/hide tips panel |
| `Space` | Toggle camera (when not typing) |

## 📊 Understanding Your Scores

### Confidence (0-100)
How confident and assertive your answer appears:
- **80-100**: Excellent - Strong, confident responses
- **60-79**: Good - Solid answers with room for improvement
- **Below 60**: Needs work - Practice being more assertive

### Clarity (0-100)
How clear and structured your answer is:
- **80-100**: Excellent - Well-structured, easy to follow
- **60-79**: Good - Clear but could be more organized
- **Below 60**: Needs work - Work on structure and clarity

### Relevance (0-100)
How well your answer addresses the question:
- **80-100**: Excellent - Directly answers the question
- **60-79**: Good - Addresses the question but could be tighter
- **Below 60**: Needs work - Stay focused on the question

### Eye Contact (0-100)
How consistently you maintain eye contact:
- **80-100**: Excellent - Great engagement
- **60-79**: Good - Mostly maintains contact
- **Below 60**: Needs work - Look at the camera more
