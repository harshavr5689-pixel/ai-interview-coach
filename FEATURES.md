# AI Interview Coach - Complete Features List

## 🎥 Core Interview Features

### Real-Time Webcam Analysis
- ✅ Live video feed with camera controls
- ✅ Face detection and tracking
- ✅ Mirror view for natural interaction
- ✅ Video quality settings (360p, 720p, 1080p)
- ✅ Optional video recording for playback

### AI-Powered Behavioral Analysis
- ✅ **Eye Contact Tracking** - Monitors if you're looking at the camera
- ✅ **Posture Analysis** - Evaluates sitting position and body language
- ✅ **Facial Expression Detection** - Tracks emotional engagement and confidence
- ✅ **Speech Analysis** - Analyzes pace, clarity, and volume
- ✅ Real-time score updates during interview
- ✅ Instant feedback and suggestions

### Speech & Audio Features
- ✅ **Speech Recognition** - Automatic transcription of your answers
- ✅ **Audio Level Monitoring** - Visual feedback on microphone levels
- ✅ **Filler Word Detection** - Identifies "um", "uh", "like", etc.
- ✅ **Speech Pace Analysis** - Words per minute calculation
- ✅ **Pause Detection** - Tracks natural pauses vs awkward silences
