## Getting Started

### First Time Setup

1. **Allow Camera and Microphone Access**
   - Your browser will ask for permission
   - Click "Allow" for both camera and microphone
   - These permissions are required for the app to work

2. **Check Your Environment**
   - ✅ Quiet room with minimal background noise
   - ✅ Good lighting (face clearly visible)
   - ✅ Camera at eye level
   - ✅ Stable internet connection (for speech recognition)

3. **Test Your Equipment**
   - Ensure your webcam shows a clear image
   - Speak a few words to test microphone
   - Check audio levels in the visualizer

## During the Interview

### Understanding the Interface

**Main Video Feed**
- Shows your webcam feed (mirrored)
- Recording indicator (red dot) when active
- Camera/mic controls at bottom

**Question Card**
- Current question displayed prominently
- Question category badge (Behavioral, Technical, etc.)
- Follow-up questions you might be asked
- Tips for answering this type of question

**Real-Time Feedback Panel**
- Eye Contact: Are you looking at the camera?
- Posture: Sitting position and body language
- Expression: Facial engagement and confidence
- Speech: Audio clarity and pace

**Progress Tracker**
- Questions completed vs total
- Time elapsed
- Average time per question
- Estimated time remaining

**Tips Panel**
- Category-specific guidance
- STAR method reminders
- Best practices

### Answering Questions

**Step 1: Read the Question**
- Take time to understand what's being asked
- Note the question category
- Review any follow-up questions listed

**Step 2: Think Before Speaking**
- It's okay to take 10-20 seconds to think
- Organize your thoughts
- Consider using STAR method for behavioral questions

**Step 3: Start Recording Your Answer**
- Click "Start Answer" or press `Ctrl + S`
- Speak clearly and at a moderate pace
- Look at the camera (imagine talking to a person)
- Watch the real-time feedback

**Step 4: Complete Your Answer**
- Aim for 2-3 minutes per answer
- Provide specific examples
- Conclude with results or learnings
- Click "Stop Answer" or press `Ctrl + E`

**Step 5: Move to Next Question**
- Review your transcript if needed
- Click "Next Question" or press `Ctrl + N`
- Take a brief pause between questions if needed

### Using Keyboard Shortcuts

- `Ctrl + S` - Start recording
- `Ctrl + E` - Stop recording
- `Ctrl + N` - Next question
- `Ctrl + →` - Skip question

### Monitoring Your Performance

**Green Scores (80-100%)**: Excellent! Keep it up!
**Yellow Scores (60-79%)**: Good, with room for improvement
**Red Scores (Below 60%)**: Needs attention

**Real-Time Adjustments:**
- Low eye contact? Look more at the camera
- Poor posture? Sit up straighter
- Low audio? Speak louder or closer to mic
- Speaking too fast? Slow down and breathe

## Understanding Your Results

### Overall Performance Grade

- **A+ (95-100%)**: Outstanding performance
- **A (90-94%)**: Excellent
- **B (80-89%)**: Very Good
- **C (70-79%)**: Good
- **D (60-69%)**: Satisfactory
- **Below 60%**: Needs Improvement

### Performance Metrics Breakdown

**Eye Contact (Important!)**
- Shows confidence and engagement
- Aim for 70-80% eye contact
- Natural to look away occasionally when thinking

**Body Language**
- Posture affects perception of confidence
- Sit upright but relaxed
- Avoid excessive fidgeting

**Facial Expressions**
- Show appropriate emotion
- Smile when discussing achievements
- Show thoughtfulness for challenges

**Speech Quality**
- Clarity and pace of speaking
- Minimize filler words (um, uh, like)
- Maintain steady volume

### Session Statistics

- **Questions Answered**: Total completed
- **Total Time**: Duration of session
- **Average Response Time**: Per question
- **Best Performance**: Highest scoring question

### Question-by-Question Analysis

Each question shows:
- Your answer text
- Response time
- Individual scores (behavior, eye contact, confidence, speech)
- Comparison to your average

### Recommendations

Personalized suggestions based on:
- Areas scoring below 70%
- Patterns across questions
- Common interview best practices

**Common Recommendations:**
- Improve eye contact consistency
- Work on posture and body language
- Reduce filler words
- Provide more detailed examples
- Better use of STAR method

### Performance Timeline

Visual graph showing:
- Score progression across questions
- Trend (improving vs declining)
- Best and worst performances
- Consistency analysis

## Tips for Success

### Before the Interview

**Technical Preparation**
- Test equipment beforehand
- Close unnecessary applications
- Ensure stable internet
- Have a backup device ready

**Mental Preparation**
- Review company values
- Prepare example stories
- Practice common questions
- Get adequate rest

**Physical Setup**
- Clean, professional background
- Good lighting (face fully lit)
- Remove distractions
- Have notes nearby (if needed)

### During the Interview

**Communication Best Practices**

1. **Use the STAR Method for Behavioral Questions**
   - **S**ituation: Set the context
   - **T**ask: Explain your responsibility
   - **A**ction: Describe what you did
   - **R**esult: Share the outcome

2. **For Technical Questions**
   - Clarify requirements first
   - Think out loud
   - Consider edge cases
   - Optimize your solution
   - Test with examples

3. **Body Language Tips**
   - Maintain eye contact (70-80%)
   - Sit up straight but relaxed
   - Use natural hand gestures
   - Show enthusiasm through expressions
   - Avoid crossing arms

4. **Speaking Tips**
   - Speak at moderate pace (130-150 wpm)
   - Pause instead of using filler words
   - Vary your tone to show engagement
   - Breathe deeply before answering
   - Project confidence in your voice

### After Each Session

**Review and Learn**
- Study your results dashboard
- Note improvement areas
- Celebrate strengths
- Plan focused practice
- Track progress over time

**Practice Regularly**
- Do quick sessions weekly
- Full sessions before real interviews
- Focus on weak areas
- Build confidence gradually
- Track improvement trends

## Troubleshooting

### Camera Issues

**Camera not working?**
- Check browser permissions
- Close other apps using camera
- Try different browser
- Restart browser/computer
- Check camera privacy settings in Windows

**Poor video quality?**
- Improve lighting
- Clean camera lens
- Check internet speed
- Adjust video quality in settings
- Move closer to light source

### Microphone Issues

**No audio detected?**
- Check browser permissions
- Select correct microphone in system settings
- Test in another application
- Adjust microphone sensitivity
- Check physical mute button

**Low audio levels?**
- Speak closer to microphone
- Increase system volume
- Check app permissions
- Reduce background noise
- Use external microphone

### Speech Recognition Issues

**Transcription not working?**
- Check internet connection (required)
- Allow microphone access
- Speak clearly and at moderate pace
- Ensure English language setting
- Try Chrome browser (best support)

**Inaccurate transcription?**
- Speak more clearly
- Reduce background noise
- Slow down speaking pace
- Enunciate words
- Check microphone quality

### Performance Issues

**App running slowly?**
- Close unnecessary browser tabs
- Clear browser cache
- Restart browser
- Update browser to latest version
- Check system resources

**Video lag or freezing?**
- Close other applications
- Check internet speed
- Lower video quality in settings
- Restart the session
- Try different browser

### Browser Compatibility

**Best experience:**
- Chrome (recommended)
- Edge
- Firefox

**Limited support:**
- Safari (some features may not work)
- Older browsers

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is my data saved online?**
A: No, all data is stored locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you explicitly choose to export.

**Q: Can I use this on mobile?**
A: The app is responsive but works best on desktop/laptop with a proper webcam.

**Q: How accurate is the AI analysis?**
A: The AI provides helpful feedback, but should be used as a practice tool, not definitive judgment.

**Q: Can I review past sessions?**
A: Currently, only the most recent session is available. Export your results to save them permanently.

**Q: Does this replace real interview practice?**
A: No, it's a supplement. Practice with real people too!

**Q: How often should I practice?**
A: 2-3 times per week for optimal improvement.
